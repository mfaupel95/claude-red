# Social-engineering awareness: tabletop and prevention

This skill supports awareness planning and defensive control review. Do not impersonate staff or vendors, contact real employees with deceptive pretexts, attempt facility entry, clone badges, bypass locks, plant devices, or collect credentials.

## Tabletop exercise workflow

1. Obtain written approval from security, privacy, facilities, human resources, and communications owners.
2. Use fictional identities, synthetic messages, and facilitator-led scenarios. Do not send messages or place calls to real people as part of this document's procedure.
3. Define learning goals: verify unusual requests, use known-good contact channels, report suspicious activity, and protect visitor access.
4. Discuss how staff should respond to requests involving payment, password resets, MFA approvals, sensitive documents, physical access, or removable media.
5. Record only aggregate exercise outcomes. Avoid employee rankings, sensitive personal details, or recordings of real conversations.
6. Debrief participants, provide reporting channels, and document control improvements without blame.

## Defensive control review

- Verify visitor registration, escort, badge return, and lost-badge reporting procedures through policy review and tabletop discussion.
- Review help-desk identity verification and out-of-band approval processes using synthetic cases.
- Confirm removable-media controls, device inventory, endpoint protections, and incident reporting.
- Assess public-facing contact procedures and staff training without gathering personal data or testing real individuals.
- Ensure facilities and security teams can escalate suspicious requests and preserve relevant evidence.

## Safe exercise boundaries

Use a tabletop or announced walk-through with no entry attempts and no interaction that could deceive, pressure, or endanger staff. Any live exercise requires a separate written scope, participant safeguards, facilities approval, stop criteria, and a non-invasive design. Do not use badge readers, lock tools, planted devices, covert recording, or credential capture.
